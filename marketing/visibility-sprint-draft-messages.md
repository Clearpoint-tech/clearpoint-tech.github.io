## LinkedIn post 2 — checklist angle

Before an email fraud incident, most companies assume email is fine because it works.

A few practical questions are worth checking:

- Which systems are allowed to send email for your domain?
- Is DKIM signing enabled?
- Does DMARC exist, and is it only monitoring?
- Are newsletter, CRM, website and invoicing tools aligned?
- Would your team verify changed bank details outside email?

## LinkedIn post 3 — provider-ready angle

A common SME email-security problem is not that nobody cares.

It is that nobody owns the complete picture.

The email provider handles mailboxes. The web agency handles the domain. A newsletter tool sends campaigns. The invoicing tool sends invoices. The CRM sends notifications.

Then nobody is fully sure whether SPF, DKIM and DMARC are complete.

Clearpoint's Email Security Health Check is designed to make that first picture clear:

- visible email-domain configuration
- business impact summary
- evidence-based findings
- provider-ready next steps
- no passwords or mailbox access

Useful when you want a practical fix list before starting a larger security project.
