<section class="section" aria-labelledby="work-title">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-head">
      <h2 id="work-title">How I work</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="split">
      <div>
        <p>Work starts by clarifying the problem before touching systems or documents:</p>
        <ul class="plain-list">
          <li>what needs to be solved, and why now;</li>
          <li>what risk or friction it creates;</li>
          <li>which systems and stakeholders are involved;</li>
          <li>what a useful next step looks like;</li>
          <li>what is realistic with the resources available.</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div>
        <p>A few principles stay constant:</p>
        <ul class="plain-list">
          <li><strong>Proportionate</strong>: effort matches the actual risk.</li>
          <li><strong>Independent</strong>: advice is not tied to selling a product or vendor package.</li>
          <li><strong>Documented</strong>: decisions, ownership, and next steps are written down.</li>
          <li><strong>Shared</strong>: management and technical teams work from the same picture.</li>
        </ul>
        <p class="muted">For larger or more specialised engagements, additional expertise can be brought in from a trusted network, while scope, coordination, and accountability stay clear.</p>
      </div>
