## Independent technical advisory for clearer, safer decisions

Clearpoint is an independent technical advisory practice for Swiss SMEs and growing organisations that need practical help across cybersecurity, AI-assisted automation, and technical project delivery.

The aim is simple: reduce avoidable risk, remove operational friction, and turn unclear technical questions into decisions and next steps that management and technical teams can both understand.

Clearpoint is led by **Philip Ritter** and is designed for situations where security, tooling, workflows, stakeholders, and implementation need to be handled together — not passed between disconnected boxes.

## How Clearpoint works

Clearpoint can support a short advisory question, a focused assessment, or a more hands-on implementation and delivery engagement.

Typical work starts by clarifying:

- what problem needs to be solved,
- what risk or friction it creates,
- what systems and stakeholders are involved,
- what a useful next step looks like,
- what can be done pragmatically with the resources available.
