## Privacy

Personal data may be processed when users contact Clearpoint by email, book an initial conversation, or submit a service request form. Such data is used solely to respond to requests, prepare or provide services, and handle related communication.

Some request, booking, or payment-related flows may be provided through external service providers such as Tally, Google Calendar, Stripe, or similar tools where applicable. Please do not submit passwords, API keys, confidential files, customer data, mailbox exports, logs, screenshots, or other sensitive material through standard forms or booking tools. If sensitive information is needed later, a safer handling method can be agreed separately.

Personal data is handled in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).
