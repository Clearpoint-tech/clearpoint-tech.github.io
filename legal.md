Philip Ritter operates Clearpoint from Switzerland and is responsible for the processing of personal data described here. For privacy questions or requests, please write to [contact@clearpoint.ch](mailto:contact@clearpoint.ch).

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics.

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. When you access the site, GitHub may process technical connection data, such as your IP address and request information, to deliver and secure the service. This processing is governed by [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and may take place outside Switzerland.

### Email and booking

You can contact Clearpoint by email or book a conversation through an external [Google Calendar booking page]({{ site.booking_url }}). In both cases, Clearpoint receives the contact and scheduling details you provide, such as your name, email address, organisation, preferred time, and any message you include. These details are used only to respond to your inquiry and to arrange and prepare conversations.

Bookings are handled by Google under its own privacy terms. Google may process this data outside Switzerland.

### Retention

Personal data is kept only as long as needed for your inquiry or any resulting working relationship, or longer where applicable legal obligations require it.

### Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), you can ask what personal data Clearpoint holds about you and request that it be corrected or deleted, within the limits of the law. Please send such requests to [contact@clearpoint.ch](mailto:contact@clearpoint.ch).

### Sensitive information

Please do not send passwords, API keys, confidential files, customer data, mailbox exports, logs, screenshots, or other sensitive material by email or through the booking tool. If sensitive information is needed later, a safer handling method can be agreed separately.
