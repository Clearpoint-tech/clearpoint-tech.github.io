## Typical topics

- A cybersecurity concern, suspicious email, or hardening question
- An SME security assessment or prioritised action plan
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC, phishing, or email security review
- A repetitive workflow that could be automated with low-code tools or AI assistance
- AI-agent, prompt engineering, or AI-workflow ideas that need practical structure
- A technical project that needs clearer structure or coordination
- A decision where business priorities and technical trade-offs need to be connected

---

## Format

- 30 minutes
- Remote video call
- No obligation
- Suitable for an initial advisory discussion or project scoping

---

## Before the call

If possible, prepare a short description of:

- what triggered the need,
- what systems, tools, or teams are involved,
- what outcome would be useful,
- any urgency, constraints, or deadlines.

Do not send passwords, API keys, confidential logs, or sensitive files through the booking form. If sensitive information is needed later, we can agree on a safer way to handle it.
